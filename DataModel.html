StageForge — Data Model

Draft v0.5 — companion to PRD v0.8
Last updated: 18 Aug 2026
Draft — schema not final

A first entity model built directly on the decisions locked into the PRD: multi-tenancy resolved as company → department with project-scoped access underneath it, and the gate-closure mechanism with its two governed escape hatches — PM bypass and SRO override.

01Scope

This covers the Phase 1 MVP core (tenancy, stage-gate, deliverables, evidence) plus the Compliance/SRO mechanism in full, since that's now fully specified even though it lands in Phase 2. Resourcing and Finance entities are sketched only, at the bottom, since they're Phase 2–3 and thin on detail in the PRD.

02Tenancy & access

The PRD's worked example showed that "tenant" can't mean "the company that owns the project" — a project is inherently shared live across at least the FM Contractor and the Client Authority. The schema resolves this by never linking a Company or Department directly to a Project. Access is granted through a join record, scoped per project, per department, per role.

EntityPurposeKey fields
CompanyTop-tier tenant — an FM Contractor or Client Authority organisation.id, name, type
DepartmentSecond-tier tenant nested under a Company — a business unit, region, or department with its own budget/Sponsor.id, company_id, name
UserAn individual person; belongs to a home Department but can be granted roles on any project they're assigned to.id, name, email, home_department_id
RolePermission definition. The seven core roles (PM, Sponsor, SRO, FM Contractor, Client Authority, Compliance Officer, Resource/Portfolio Manager, Finance) are global; a SectorVariant can also define its own additional roles.id, name, permissions, sector_variant_id (nullable)
ProjectRoleAssignmentThe access join. Grants one User (from one Department) one Role on one Project. This is the entity that actually resolves the tenancy question.id, project_id, department_id, user_id, role_id
DecidedConfirmed: a sector variant can introduce its own role beyond the core seven. Modelled with a nullable sector_variant_id on Role — null means the role is global and available to every sector; set means it's specific to that sector and only assignable on projects within it.
DecidedConfirmed: template authoring (StageTemplate, ComplianceRuleSet) is restricted to Platform Admin and Compliance Officer. Platform Admin is a genuinely different shape of role from the other eight — it isn't assigned per ProjectRoleAssignment at all, since template content isn't project-scoped. It's a tenant- or platform-level permission, sitting outside this table.

03Gate closure mechanism

The PRD's governance decisions add up to an AND condition: a gate can only move to Sponsor approval once both the delivery checklist and the compliance checklist are clear — where "clear" means either genuinely satisfied, or satisfied through one of the two governed escape hatches, each locked to a specific role and each leaving a reason on the record.

Delivery

EntityPurposeKey fields
SectorVariantHealth, Defence, Justice, etc. — determines which template libraries and branding apply.id, name, brand_name
StageTemplateConfigurable library entry: an ordered set of gates and deliverables for a programme type, scoped to a sector. Authored by Platform Admin or Compliance Officer only.id, sector_variant_id, name
ProjectThe central unit. Carries the central project number everything else attaches to. A PM may scope it to a subset of its StageTemplate's stages, and edit that selection later — e.g. reinstating a stage if scope grows mid-programme.id, project_number, sector_variant_id, stage_template_id, included_stage_keys, name
StageAn instance of a StageTemplate applied to a Project, in sequence. Only instantiated for stages in Project.included_stage_keys.id, project_id, template_id, order
GateThe checkpoint ending a Stage.id, stage_id, status
DeliverableA checklist item instance at a Gate. Carries whether it's legally significant, from its template.id, gate_id, status, legally_significant
EvidenceFileAn uploaded file against a Deliverable, versioned.id, deliverable_id, version, uploaded_by, uploaded_at
DeliverableBypassRecord of bypassing a deliverable's evidence requirement. bypassed_by must hold PM, Compliance Officer, or SRO — PM only if the deliverable isn't legally significant.id, deliverable_id, bypassed_by, reason (required), timestamp
GateSignOffRecord of the Sponsor's approval.id, gate_id, signed_off_by, timestamp
DecidedConfirmed: a deliverable bypass requires a reason, for traceability — mandatory, not optional. Every override/bypass in this model now carries the same requirement.
DecidedConfirmed: a PM cannot bypass a deliverable that carries legal weight — that escalates to Compliance Officer or SRO instead. Bypass authority is set per deliverable (via its template's legally_significant flag), not uniformly PM-level.

Compliance & governance

EntityPurposeKey fields
ComplianceRuleSetConfig-driven rule library, scoped to a SectorVariant and jurisdiction.id, sector_variant_id, name
ComplianceRequirementAn instance of a rule attached to a Stage — the compliance equivalent of a Deliverable.id, stage_id, status, blocks_gate
ComplianceOverrideRecord of an SRO overriding every outstanding compliance requirement on a Gate, in one action.id, gate_id, overridden_by, reason (required), covered_requirement_ids, timestamp
AuditLogEntryAppend-only trail. One row per upload, bypass, override, sign-off, and rejection, across every entity above.id, actor_id, action, entity_type, entity_id, timestamp
DecidedConfirmed: an SRO override clears everything outstanding on the Gate in one action — not a per-requirement record. covered_requirement_ids snapshots which specific requirements were outstanding at the moment of override, so the audit trail shows exactly what the SRO accepted, even if new requirements are added to the stage later.

05Resolved this round

Decided Programme is not a real entity for now — confirmed, though it could become one as project scope grows, so Project is deliberately left without a programme_id today rather than a real one being added and left unused — the field should be added only once there's an actual grouping need, as a nullable FK, so it's a non-breaking addition when that day comes.

Also resolved: sector-specific roles, mandatory bypass reasons, and single-action SRO overrides — folded into the tables above rather than repeated here.
