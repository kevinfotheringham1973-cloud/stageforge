# Lift & Vertical Transportation — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Verdict: Needs fixes

## Completeness findings
- **Genuinely time-sensitive standards transition not yet reflected.** Verified via WebSearch (multiple industry sources — Elevator World, TÜV SÜD, ELA): **BS EN ISO 8100-1/-2 is replacing BS EN 81-20/-50** as the international lift safety standard. Publication was targeted for March 2026, with harmonization expected around Q3 2026 — i.e. **this is happening right now, in the same year this checklist would be used for a live procurement.** There is a 36-month transition period during which EN 81-20/-50 certifications remain valid, but any lift package specified or tendered from mid-2026 onward may reference either standard depending on the manufacturer's certification timeline. The checklist's Table 0 guidance and Stage 4/6 rows only cite "BS EN 81 series" — recommend adding a note that BS EN ISO 8100-1 (design/installation) and 8100-2 (component calculation/testing/documentation) are the incoming replacement standards, so a PM isn't caught out by a 2027+ tender referencing the new numbering.
- **BS 5655-6 citation should be re-verified before reuse.** The guidance table cites "BS 5655-6 (Selection and installation of new lifts)". Search results show this standard has been through multiple supersessions (2002 edition superseded by a 2011 edition); given the broader EN 81 → ISO 8100 transition happening the same year, recommend simplifying the guidance table to rely on BS EN 81 series / the incoming ISO 8100 standards as the primary construction/installation reference, and either drop the BS 5655-6 citation or confirm its exact current edition before keeping it.

## Redundancy / unnecessary-step findings
None found. The firefighting/escape-lift-specific rows (SFPN 3, BS EN 81-72) are correctly scoped as conditional ("if applicable") rather than forced onto every project, and the LOLER cross-stage reference is correctly placed under Stage 7 (Use) as an ongoing statutory requirement rather than duplicated across every stage.

## Citations checked
- **BS EN ISO 8100-1/-2 transition** — confirmed via WebSearch (Elevator World, TÜV SÜD press release Nov 2025, ELA comparison document): publication ~March 2026, harmonization ~Q3 2026, 36-month transition window. This is new information not previously known to this project.
- **BS 5655-6** — confirmed superseded at least once (2002→2011); current live status not conclusively confirmed, flagged for a follow-up check rather than asserted either way.
- Lift Regulations / Lifts Directive, BS EN 81-72 (firefighting lifts) — no issues found.
