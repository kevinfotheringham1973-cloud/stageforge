# Fire Suppression Systems — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Verdict: Needs fixes (minor)

## Completeness findings
- **Same SHTM 85 omission as the Fire Alarm & Detection checklist.** The guidance table cites SHTM 81, 82, 86, 00 but not **SHTM 85 — "Fire precautions in existing healthcare premises"** (confirmed current via direct fetch of nss.nhs.scot, not archived). Since fire suppression replacement/upgrade projects are near-universally retrofits into existing occupied hospitals, SHTM 85 belongs alongside SHTM 81 in the guidance table and Stage 1 survey row. This is the same finding as the Fire Alarm & Detection checklist — the two fire-domain checklists share the same gap, worth fixing together for consistency.

## Redundancy / unnecessary-step findings
None found. Sequencing is sound (hazard classification → hydraulic/agent calculations → cause & effect interface with the Fire Alarm system → commissioning discharge verification → ongoing interface testing). The cross-references to SHTM 82 (fire alarm interface) are genuine interface-management content, not duplication of that system's own checklist.

## Citations checked
- **SHTM 83 is not cited in this file at all except once in Stage 6's training row ("SHTM 81, SHTM 83")** — correctly used, since SHTM 83 is confirmed real/current (see Fire Alarm & Detection audit for the primary-source verification; a prior project memory incorrectly claimed SHTM 83 doesn't exist — that claim is wrong).
- BS EN 12845 (sprinklers) and BS EN 15004 / ISO 14520 (gaseous suppression) — no evidence of supersession found; both remain the standard current references for these system types.
