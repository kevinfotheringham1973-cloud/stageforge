# Chilled Water & Cooling Systems Replacement — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Source: `Chilled water  cooling systems.docx`

## Verdict: Needs fixes (one significant gap, one terminology issue)

## Completeness findings
1. **Legionella/ACOP L8 control is entirely absent, despite this checklist explicitly covering cooling systems that can include evaporative cooling towers ("free cooling" is named in the Key Principles).** Evaporative cooling towers/condensers are one of the best-documented Legionella risk vectors in UK building services (several serious/fatal outbreaks have been traced to poorly-managed cooling towers), and there is a specific legal duty to **notify the local authority of a notifiable cooling tower/evaporative condenser** (Notification of Cooling Towers and Evaporative Condensers Regulations 1992, as amended) in addition to the general HSE ACOP L8/HSG274 water-hygiene regime. The file's Stage 7 "water treatment programme and monitoring procedures" is the only place this is even implicitly touched, with no named regulation. Recommend adding: (a) a Stage 4 design deliverable for Legionella/biocide dosing regime specific to any wet cooling tower/evaporative element (distinct from the closed-loop chilled-water-circuit water treatment already covered), (b) a Stage 6/7 deliverable confirming local-authority cooling-tower notification where applicable, citing HSE ACOP L8/HSG274 and the 1992 Regulations. This is the single most consequential finding in this batch — a real infection-control/legal-compliance gap, not a stylistic one.
2. **Stage 4 — same generic "Building Regulations / statutory compliance information" terminology issue found across this batch** (see `boiler-heating-plant.md`).
