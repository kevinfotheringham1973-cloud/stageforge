# Room, Ward or Theatre Refresh (incl. MHU Ligature) — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Verdict: Needs fixes

## Completeness findings
- **No explicit asbestos refurbishment & demolition (R&D) survey deliverable.** Asbestos is mentioned only once, in passing, inside the Stage 1 "Initial risk register (ligature, infection, clinical disruption, fire, asbestos)" line — there is no standalone deliverable requiring a CAR 2012-compliant R&D survey before any fabric-disturbing work (flooring removal, wall cladding, door/window replacement). This system is exactly the kind of work — internal fabric disturbance in likely PFI-era-or-older hospital buildings — where CAR 2012 Regulation 5 requires this survey before work starts, not just a risk-register mention. Recommend adding an explicit Stage 1 (or Stage 4, immediately before Stage 5 works) deliverable: "Asbestos refurbishment & demolition survey obtained and reviewed prior to any fabric-disturbing works — CAR 2012."

## Redundancy / unnecessary-step findings
None found.
