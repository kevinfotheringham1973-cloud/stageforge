# Boiler & Heating Plant Replacement — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Source: `Boiler_template_V2.0.docx`

## Verdict: Needs fixes (one systemic terminology issue, otherwise strong)

## Completeness findings
1. **Stage 4 — "Building Regulations / statutory compliance information" uses the wrong national terminology.** This is a Scottish NHS PFI2 project, and Scotland's building-control regime is the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 / Building (Scotland) Regulations 2004, administered via **Building Warrant** and (for existing PFI-occupied buildings) **Schedule 3 (existing buildings)** compliance — not "Building Regulations," which is the England & Wales term. This file (and 5 others in this batch — BMS, Chilled Water, Compressed Air, Electrical, Electronic Communications) all use the generic term. Given this project's own established discipline of always naming the Scotland-specific instrument (SHTM not HTM, Building Standards (Scotland) elsewhere, Scottish Water Byelaws in the Domestic Water file), this is a real, fixable inconsistency — recommend "Building Warrant / Building (Scotland) Regulations 2004 compliance information (Schedule 3 where the works affect an existing occupied building)".
2. Everything else is genuinely thorough: the Pre-Contract Hold Point governance gate is explicit and well-sequenced (two competitive quotes → PFI Board cost approval → Water Safety Group SBAR approval, all required before Stage 5 can start), DSEAR (fuel supply/combustion air/hot-works) and Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 are both present at Stage 4, PSSR written-scheme updates are in the Cross-Stage section, and PVG/RPA cross-cutting lines are both present and correctly worded.

## Redundancy / unnecessary-step findings
None found.
