# Renewable / Net Zero Plant — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Verdict: Needs fixes (minor)

## Completeness findings
- **Citation precision, Stage 0/1/7**: "Climate Emergency & Sustainability Strategy" is close but not the exact citable title. Verified via WebSearch: the real document is **"NHS Scotland Climate Emergency and Sustainability Strategy: 2022-2026"** (published by the Scottish Government, launched August 2022, targets net zero by 2040). This project's own established discipline (SHPN 16-01 v2.0 Nov 2017, BS 6229:2025, etc.) is to cite the full title plus edition/date so a PM can actually go find the document — this citation should be tightened to the full name and edition range rather than left as a generic paraphrase.
- No other completeness gaps. This is otherwise the most thoroughly staged of the 8 files in this batch: DNO capacity/G98/G99 connection process is correctly staged from a preliminary headroom check (Stage 0) through formal pre-application (Stage 1), indicative route (Stage 2), formal application+offer (Stage 4), pre-energisation approval (Stage 5), and finalised agreement (Stage 6) — a realistic multi-gate grid-connection process, not compressed into one step. BESS DSEAR/fire risk is correctly staged from scoping (Stage 1) through a finalised risk assessment (Stage 4), installation verification (Stage 5), and an explicit *periodic* review cycle rather than a one-off Stage 4 exercise (Cross-Stage) — this last point is a genuinely good practice catch, since a one-off BESS fire-risk assessment would be a real gap for a system with an evolving hazard profile. Correctly verifies the heat pump/PV retrofit doesn't compromise SHTM 03-01 ventilation requirements (Stage 4) — directly reflects this project's own prior finding (`nhs-scotland-netzero-energy` agent's core concern) that energy retrofits can conflict with clinical ventilation minimums.

## Redundancy / unnecessary-step findings
None found. Stage 5's "PVG-checked contractor attendance records" line is a stage-specific echo of the universal Cross-Stage PVG bullet — not true duplication, it reinforces the requirement at the exact point (live electrical isolation work) where it matters operationally most. Consistent in spirit with Automatic Doors' extra RPA staging (see that report) — an intentional depth choice, not an inconsistency to correct.
