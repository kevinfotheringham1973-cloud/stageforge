# Catering / Kitchen Equipment — PM Guidance Audit (2026-09-10)

## Verdict: Needs fixes

## Completeness findings
- **Real citation error, Guidance table ("Water-Using Equipment" row) and nowhere else in the file**: cites "Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations" for warewasher/ice-machine/combi-oven backflow prevention. Verified via WebSearch: the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999 apply only to **England and Wales**. Scotland's equivalent, current instrument is the **Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws 2014** (in force since 12 July 2014, replacing the 2004 byelaws), enforced by Scottish Water. WRAS itself is a UK-wide approval/product-certification scheme so that part of the citation is fine — only the named "Regulations" is wrong for a Scottish site. Same class of error the parallel Water Treatment Plant audit found independently. Fix: change the Guidance-table citation to "Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws 2014 / WRAS backflow prevention" — Stage 4/6 rows that cite bare "WRAS" don't need changing, only the one row that names the wrong instrument.
- No other completeness gaps found. The rest of the lifecycle is thorough: gas/electric split and Food Standards Scotland interface (Stage 0-1), cooking-line fire-suppression concept correctly cross-referenced to the Fire Suppression Systems Replacement checklist rather than duplicated (Stage 2), full technical design covering gas safety/fire-suppression interlock/F-Gas/backflow/electrical (Stage 4), Gas Safe-registered installation testing (Stage 5), and an explicit ongoing DW/172-172/TR19 duct-cleaning + annual gas-safety regime (Stage 6-7).
