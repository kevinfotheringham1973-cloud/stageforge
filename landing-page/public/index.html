What StageForge Health does

Built for teams delivering hospital estates projects under real regulatory pressure — where inconsistent PM skill, complex overlapping regulations, and no shared system across projects is the actual, everyday problem.

1

A template library, not a blank page

Every project starts from a discipline-specific template — Boiler, UPS, Water Systems, Fire Detection and more — each mapped from real RIBA/SHTM/HTM-aligned source documents, so consistency doesn't depend on which PM happens to be running it.

2

Compliance runs with delivery, not after it

Compliance items sit alongside the deliverables they belong to, with tiered override authority for anything carrying legal weight — a named specialist (Fire Officer, an Authorised Person, Clinical Safety Officer) is an exact-match requirement even the SRO doesn't bypass.

3

Nothing closes without evidence and sign-off

A gate can't move forward without uploaded evidence and a Sponsor's sign-off against it — a real audit trail, not a status someone typed into a tracker.

The problem isn't a lack of process — it's no control or consistency across projects, layered with complex regulation and variable PM skill. A template library that already encodes the right stages, deliverables and compliance checks is the answer.
